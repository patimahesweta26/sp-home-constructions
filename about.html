Who We Are

A small family venture that grew into Bhubaneswar's trusted name

SP Home Constructions started with one simple belief: build every home as if it were our own. From that first project in Khandagiri, we've grown into a construction and repair company that homeowners across Bhubaneswar rely on — without ever losing our family warmth.

We specialize in residential construction, renovations, and property maintenance. From modern new-home builds to emergency repairs, our team brings the same care and precision to every project — built on transparency, integrity, and a deep commitment to client satisfaction.
