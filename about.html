Our Story

Crafting Bhubaneswar's Finest Homes for Over a Decade

SP Home Constructions started as a small family-run venture in Khandagiri, Bhubaneswar with one simple belief: build every home as if it were your own. Over the years, that belief has grown into one of the area's most trusted construction and repair companies.

We specialize in residential construction, renovations, and property maintenance — delivering quality workmanship that stands the test of Odisha's demanding climate. From modern new-home builds to emergency repairs, our team brings the same care and precision to every project.

Every project we undertake is built on transparency, integrity, and a deep commitment to client satisfaction. We don't just build structures — we build lasting relationships with every homeowner we serve.
