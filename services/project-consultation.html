FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, our initial consultation and site evaluation are completely free with no obligation. You only pay if you decide to engage us for a project.

Can you review a quotation from another contractor?

Yes, we can review and explain contractor quotations so you understand what's included and whether the pricing is fair.

Do you help with material selection?

Yes, we advise on cement, steel, sand, paints, tiles, and other materials — helping you balance quality and budget.

Can you supervise a project built by another contractor?

Yes, we offer third-party supervision and quality audit services for projects being built by other contractors.
