FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to build a home in Bhubaneswar?

Home construction costs in Bhubaneswar typically range from ₹1,600 to ₹2,800 per square foot depending on materials, design, and finishing level. Contact us for a detailed free quotation for your specific plot.

How long does a new home construction take?

A standard residential build in Bhubaneswar takes 8 to 14 months depending on size and complexity. We provide a clear timeline before work begins and deliver on schedule.

Do you provide building permits and approvals?

Yes, we help you obtain all required local building approvals and permits from the Bhubaneswar Development Authority (BDA) as part of our project management service.

Can I customize the house design?

Absolutely. We work with you and our design team to tailor the plan to your lifestyle, budget, and land — from floor plan to finishing touches.
