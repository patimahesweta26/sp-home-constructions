FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does waterproofing last?

With quality materials and proper application, roof and terrace waterproofing typically lasts 5 to 10 years. We also provide workmanship warranties.

Is waterproofing done from inside or outside?

It depends on the area. Roofs and terraces are waterproofed from the outside, while bathrooms and basements often use internal membranes and coatings. We recommend the most effective method for your situation.

How long does the waterproofing process take?

A typical terrace or roof waterproofing job takes 2 to 5 days including surface preparation and curing. Bathroom waterproofing takes 1 to 2 days.

Can you fix an existing leakage problem?

Yes. We first locate and repair the source of the leak, then apply proper waterproofing treatment so the problem doesn't return.
