FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a home renovation take in Bhubaneswar?

A single room or kitchen renovation takes 2 to 4 weeks, while a full home remodel takes 2 to 4 months depending on scope. We give you a clear timeline upfront.

Can I stay in my home during renovation?

Yes, for most projects we phase the work room by room so you can stay in your home with minimal disruption. For major structural work we'll advise the best approach.

Do you handle demolition and debris removal?

Yes, our renovation packages include demolition, debris removal, and site cleanup as standard.

Can you renovate an old house to modern standards?

Yes — modernizing older homes is one of our specialties, including rewiring, new plumbing, waterproofing, and contemporary interiors.
